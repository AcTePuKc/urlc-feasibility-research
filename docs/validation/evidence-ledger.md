# URLC evidence ledger

**Assessment date:** 2026-08-17  
**Decision status:** Stage 0 Pass; Gate 1 Insufficient evidence; Gates 2-4 not run.

| ID | Gate | Class | Claim or question | Evidence and status | Confidence | Limitation / contradiction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E01 | 0/1 | Fact | More QR payload can require a higher version. | DENSO capacity tables define version-, mode-, and ECC-dependent limits. [Source](https://www.qrcode.com/en/about/version.html) | High | Does not establish an operational problem. |
| E02 | 0 | Measurement | A 184-byte payload at ECC M requires V10 under single byte mode. | V9 holds 180 bytes; V10 holds 213. [Source](https://www.qrcode.com/en/about/versionPage/versionPage1_10.html) | High | Mixed segmentation may differ for a particular payload. |
| E03 | 0 | Measurement | A 92-character payload at ECC M fits V6 in byte mode or V5 in Alphanumeric mode. | DENSO capacities are 106 byte characters at V6-M and 122 Alphanumeric characters at V5-M. [Source](https://www.qrcode.com/en/about/versionPage/versionPage1_10.html) | High | Says nothing about whether URLC can produce such a payload. |
| E04 | 0/1 | Fact | Larger modules are generally more stable to scan. | DENSO advises larger module sizes; detector experiments degrade near low pixel-per-module sampling. [DENSO](https://www.qrcode.com/en/howto/cell.html), [experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9572759/) | Medium-high | No universal field effect size; many confounders. |
| E05 | 1 | Hypothesis | Long URLs materially reduce scan success in real operations. | Mechanically plausible; no representative joined payload/failure dataset located. | Low | Central Gate 1 evidence gap. |
| E06 | 1 | Measurement | Recent physical QR URLs averaged 45 characters. | QRisk: 860 codes, >93% URLs, 12-203 range, 45 mean. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-00627-1_1) | Medium | 721/860 were from France; no tail distribution or scan outcomes. |
| E07 | 1 | Measurement | Historically, payload lengths 8-128 were most common in a very large scan corpus. | 87.6M scans from one app, May 2013-March 2014. [Source](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~franzi/pdf/qr-mobisys2015.pdf) | Medium | Old, single-app vantage point, and raw data unavailable. |
| E08 | 1 | Unresolved question | What fraction of current QR-bound URLs cross consequential version or module-size constraints? | No suitable public distribution found. | High confidence that unresolved | Common Crawl is not a QR corpus; public industry datasets omit raw lengths. |
| E09 | 1 | Unresolved question | How often is QR density causal in failures, reprints, or abandonment? | No located dataset joins payload, symbol geometry, conditions, outcome, and cost. | High confidence that unresolved | Print, contrast, placement, damage, curvature, and camera may dominate. |
| E10 | 1 | Measurement | Dynamic QR/shortener use exists in physical deployments. | 235 of valid unique scans (32%) in a 948-code convenience collection. [Source](https://conpro23.ieee-security.org/papers/siddiqi-conpro23.pdf) | Medium | Pandemic-era, geographically concentrated, non-random sample. |
| E11 | 1 | Fact | A dynamic QR adds an intermediary request and enables service observation. | The study observed redirects and provider analytics; vendors advertise scan analytics. [Study](https://conpro23.ieee-security.org/papers/siddiqi-conpro23.pdf), [Bitly](https://bitly.com/pages/pricing) | High | Destination and ordinary web infrastructure still observe subsequent access. |
| E12 | 1 | Measurement | The studied QR ecosystem was not a unique or substantial privacy threat relative to normal online links. | Authors' conclusion in the 2023 study. [Source](https://conpro23.ieee-security.org/papers/siddiqi-conpro23.pdf) | Medium | Privacy expectations and provider practices vary. |
| E13 | 1 | Fact | Redirect-service retirement can break a subset of embedded links. | Google's 2025 goo.gl policy deactivated links previously classified as inactive. [Source](https://developers.googleblog.com/google-url-shortener-links-will-no-longer-be-available/) | High | Google preserved active links; does not quantify QR-specific harm. |
| E14 | 1 | Fact | Redirects also provide valued mutability and analytics. | Dynamic QR research and Bitly product documentation. [Study](https://conpro23.ieee-security.org/papers/siddiqi-conpro23.pdf), [Bitly](https://bitly.com/pages/pricing) | High | Value varies by publisher. |
| E15 | 1 | Fact | Some long-lived device labels explicitly face shortener stability/privacy trade-offs. | RFC 9238 discusses MUD URLs in QR labels and shorteners. [Source](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc9238/) | High | Establishes a niche and concern, not measured incidence or harm. |
| E16 | 1 | Fact | Persistence is not conferred solely by identifier syntax. | ARK describes persistence as a service matter; DOI relies on maintained resolution governance. [ARK](https://arks.org/specs/), [DOI](https://www.doi.org/doi-handbook/html/) | High | Local decoding can still remove one intermediary. |
| E17 | 1 | Assumption | Archives, museums, government, and long-lived asset owners would adopt URLC. | No direct stakeholder evidence in the report or located sources. | Very low | These groups may prefer controlled HTTPS domains or PIDs. |
| E18 | 1 | Unresolved question | What improvement is practically meaningful? | Must be defined per cohort in scan failures, label area, distance, reprints, cost, or risk. | High confidence that unresolved | A universal compression-percentage threshold would be arbitrary. |
| E19 | 0/2 | Fact | CRI is substantial structural binary URI prior art. | CRI encodes URI components in CBOR and supports most HTTP URIs. [Source](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-core-href/) | High | Gate 2 not executed because Gate 1 did not pass. |
| E20 | 0/2 | Fact | CRI does not promise exact lexical reconstruction. | URI-to-CRI conversion is unspecified provided the result converts to an equivalent URI. [Source](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-core-href/) | High | Whether lexical identity matters is unproved. |
| E21 | 0/2 | Fact | CRI is not yet a published RFC. | RFC-to-be 9988 is blocked in final review. [Source](https://queue.rfc-editor.org/final-review/rfc9988/) | High | Status can change; recheck before later work. |
| E22 | 0/2 | Fact | GS1 has an existing lossless compressed URI design for data carriers. | Compressed Digital Link stays a valid URL on the same domain. [Source](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/resolver/) | High | Domain-specific rather than arbitrary URL compression. |
| E23 | 0/2 | Fact | Base45 is QR-efficient binary-to-text transport, not URL compression. | RFC 9285 maps two bytes to three QR-Alphanumeric characters. [Source](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc9285) | High | Its alphabet includes URI-problematic space and `%`. |
| E24 | 0/2 | Fact | No `urlc` URI scheme is currently registered. | Current IANA registry search. [Source](https://www.iana.org/assignments/uri-schemes/uri-schemes.xhtml) | High | Name availability does not establish novelty or utility. |
| E25 | 0/3 | Fact | Unverified custom schemes have handler-hijacking and ambiguity risks. | Apple and Android platform documentation. [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/Xcode/defining-a-custom-url-scheme-for-your-app), [Android](https://developer.android.com/privacy-and-security/risks/unsafe-use-of-deeplinks) | High | Gate 3 was not executed. |
| E26 | 0/3 | Hypothesis | Destination preview can mitigate URLC's destination hiding sufficiently. | No URLC-specific user study exists. | Low | Warnings are often ignored; exact UI and platform matter. |
| E27 | 0/2 | Unresolved question | Exact lexical round-trip is a genuinely needed capability. | No identified stakeholder workflow requires it. | High confidence that unresolved | Browsers parse/navigate URLs semantically; lexical differences may be irrelevant or hazardous. |
| E28 | 0/4 | Assumption | Browser/OS vendors would adopt URLC. | No vendor commitment or standards discussion located. | Very low | Adoption cost is borne substantially by platforms and users. |
| E29 | 1 | Measurement | A large vendor dataset exists but cannot answer URL-length need publicly. | QRStuff reports a 100,000-URL sample and 101.9M 2025 scans, without raw lengths. [Source](https://www.qrstuff.com/scan-report/) | Medium | Vendor sample and methodology; raw records unavailable. |
| E30 | 1 | Unresolved question | A target owner will change workflow after ordinary alternatives are tested. | No owner interviews, commitments, or pre-registered minimum important changes. | High confidence that unresolved | Required to pass Gate 1. |
