## Interpretation

Stage 0 established a falsifiable framework and audited the initial report. Gate 1 did not establish a sufficiently significant unmet need. Under the sequential decision rules, Gates 2–4 were not evaluated and implementation was not authorised.

## Resume conditions

Gate 1 may be reopened if credible external evidence provides:

- representative records from real HTTP(S)-QR publishing workflows;
- payload, QR version/ECC, physical module size, and production context;
- measured scan failures, rework, reprints, label-area costs, or resolver incidents;
- causal evidence that URL payload density or redirect dependency materially contributed;
- negative and problem-free cases, not only selected examples;
- comparison with URL hygiene, owned HTTPS short paths, governed redirects, and persistent identifiers; and
- a stakeholder-defined operational threshold sufficient to change publishing practice.

Codec experiments, synthetic QR images, or theoretical capacity savings alone do not satisfy these conditions.
