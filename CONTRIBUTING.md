# Contributing

This repository is a suspended feasibility study, not an active codec or standards project.

Contributions are most useful when they provide:

- new primary-source evidence;
- anonymised data from real HTTP(S)-QR deployments;
- documented scan failures, rework, reprints, label constraints, or redirect-service incidents;
- relevant standards or prior art missed by the research; or
- evidence that could materially change the Gate 1 decision.

Please explain the population, collection method, dates, limitations, and connection to a specific Gate 1 claim. Preserve negative and problem-free cases. Remove live tokens, personal data, confidential destinations, and customer-identifying records unless their publication is explicitly authorised.

Speculative codec implementations, custom URI schemes, browser handlers, and compression contests are out of scope while Gate 1 remains closed. A technically interesting implementation is not evidence of need.

Opening an issue is preferable to submitting a large rewrite. State whether the contribution is a fact, measurement, hypothesis, assumption, or unresolved question and link to the primary source where possible.

By contributing repository text, you agree that it may be distributed under the repository's documentation licence. Do not submit third-party material unless you have the right to do so.
